## The entry

> A washing machine is a merry-go-round that gives your clothes a bath while they ride.
> The merry-go-round fits because the drum spins your clothes round and round through soapy water, but it leaves out that the soap does the real cleaning - the ride just makes sure every sleeve gets its turn in the wash.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the two lines above to
`/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8 text.
